How it works

  1. Write it once

    Add your pets, their routine, the vet, and house info. A readiness ring shows what sitters usually look for that's still missing.

  2. Send it to your sitter

    Leashnote makes a private iCloud link and a QR code to scan at the door. Your sitter opens it in the free app.

  3. Relax while you're away

    Check-ins with photos arrive as they happen. Changed something? Push the update with one tap.

Sharing with your sitter

How do I send a guide to my sitter?

Open the guide and tap Send to your sitter. Leashnote creates a private iCloud link and a QR code. Send the link by Messages or let your sitter scan the code. You need to be signed in to iCloud in Settings.

What does my sitter need?

An iPhone with Leashnote installed (it's free for sitters) and an iCloud account. When they open your link, the guide appears under You're sitting for.

My sitter doesn't have an iPhone.

Tap Send as PDF. You'll get a printable guide with a checklist for each day and a vet authorization letter to sign. Send it by Mail, Messages, or AirDrop, or print it.

I changed the guide. Does my sitter see it?

After you edit a shared guide, a banner appears. Tap Update sitter's guide to send the changes. Updating an already-shared guide is always free.

How do I stop sharing?

Open the guide, tap the QR code or link button, and choose Stop sharing. The sitter loses access right away, and their check-ins and photos are deleted.

Check-ins and reminders

I'm not getting check-in notifications.

Make sure notifications are on for Leashnote in Settings, then Notifications, and that you're signed in to iCloud. Check-ins also show up whenever you open the guide, and Leashnote checks in the background as a backup.

How do sitter reminders work?

On the sitter's guide, tap the bell. Their iPhone will remind them of each meal, medication, and walk for the rest of the stay. Tap the bell again to turn them off.

Leashnote Plus

What does Leashnote Plus include?

A one-time $4.99 purchase that unlocks unlimited iCloud sharing, dark mode, and Match iPhone appearance. Your first shared guide is free without it. PDF export and the sitter's side are always free.

I bought Plus but it's not showing.

Open Settings in Leashnote and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. Plus works with Family Sharing.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Your guides are stored on your device. Shared guides and check-ins are in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers and can't see your data. See the privacy policy.

Do my guides sync to my other devices?

Not yet. Guides you write live on the device you wrote them on. Only shared copies are stored in iCloud.

How do I delete everything?

Delete each guide in the app (this also stops any sharing), or delete the app. To remove iCloud data, go to Settings, then your name, then iCloud, and manage Leashnote's storage.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version: Leashnote does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and no Leashnote servers. What you write stays on your device, and anything you share is stored in your own iCloud account.

This policy explains how the Leashnote app for iPhone and iPad ("Leashnote", "the app") handles information. Leashnote is made by Dylan McCaffrey ("we", "us").

Information you enter

Guides can hold details about your pets (names, photos, feeding and medication schedules, quirks, microchip numbers), contacts (your vet, neighbors, and you), emergency information, and house details such as Wi-Fi and door codes. This information is saved on your device. We never receive it.

Sharing through iCloud

When you tap "Send to your sitter", the app saves a copy of that guide to a private area of your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and creates a share link. Only people who open the link you send, and accept it in Leashnote, can read the guide. Check-ins your sitter sends, including photos, notes, and finished tasks, are saved to the same private area of your iCloud.

This data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We, as the developer, cannot read it. When you tap "Stop sharing" or delete a shared guide, the shared copy and its check-ins and photos are deleted from iCloud, and the sitter loses access.

If you're the sitter, your name and the check-ins you send are visible to the owner who shared the guide with you.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the pictures you select. Leashnote doesn't ask for access to your photo library or camera roll.

Notifications

With your permission, Leashnote sends notifications when a sitter checks in, and sitters can turn on reminders for each task. Check-in alerts are delivered by Apple through iCloud. Task reminders are scheduled on the sitter's device. Neither passes through us.

PDF export

PDFs are created on your device. They go only where you send them, such as Messages, AirDrop, or Mail.

Purchases

Leashnote Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether you own it. We don't receive your payment details or Apple ID.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

None. Leashnote contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs, and doesn't track you across apps or websites. If you've chosen to share crash reports with developers in your device settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash data.

Children

Leashnote is a general-audience app, not directed to children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete a guide in the app to remove it from your device and, if it was shared, from iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. You can also manage Leashnote's iCloud data in Settings, then your name, then iCloud.
